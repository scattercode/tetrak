THE PROGRAM
1. Haruno-Kotobuke (To Facilitate Spring)..........A Dance Fantasy
Characters
A Lady, Noted for Her Ugliness, Wife of a Nobleman......MME. KOHARU OHARA
Her Escort, a Handsome Household Servant, Officially Representing
Her Husband..............................................MISS SUZUKO TANAKA
SCENE—On the way to the Hachiman Shrine.  TIME—1178
A Thread of the Plot
The Lady, who is secretly in love with the Servant, has chosen him to accom-
pany her to the Shrine, as her official husband.  The first time they are alone, she
tells him of her admiration and infatuation, not realizing how unsightly she is in
his eyes.  The Servant finds himself in a predicament.  The Lady is his superior;
he must obey all her wishes, and yet he feels he cannot consent to her amorous
advances, which are not to his personal liking.  In the end the Servant gets out
of the situation by convincing the Lady that he is not the lover for her.  He tells
her that she is a lady of the nobility and he the humblest of the humble, and her
proposed great sacrifice would be too condescending and too humiliating.
The music for this number is by Miss Kosetsu, voice; Misses Tomako, Natsue
and Maruko playing shamisens, and the tsu tsumi, taiko, fuye and kane played
off stage.
